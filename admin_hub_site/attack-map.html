how to read this

StateMeaning
exposedNone of the controls that matter for this technique is on.
reducedSomething makes it harder or easier to detect, but an attacker can still succeed. Several techniques can never be more than reduced.
closedA control, or a combination of controls, blocks the technique as described. An open route is a chain from an entry point to an impact that passes only through techniques that are not closed.

This is a model, and a deliberately simple one. Real controls have exclusions, break-glass accounts and misconfigurations, and real attackers combine techniques in ways a map with 23 nodes cannot show. Use it to find the gaps worth an afternoon, and to explain to someone why MFA alone is not the end of the story. Technique IDs link to MITRE ATT&CK and were checked against ATT&CK v19; the stage names are ours, not MITRE tactics.

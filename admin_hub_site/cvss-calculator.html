The base score describes how bad a flaw is in the abstract, not how urgent it is for you. A 9.8 on a system nobody can reach matters less than a 7.5 that is being exploited in the wild on your internet-facing gateway. Use the score together with exploitation status (see exploited-now) and your own exposure. This page implements CVSS 3.1 base metrics only; temporal and environmental metrics and CVSS 4.0 are not covered.

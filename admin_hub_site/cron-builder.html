cron-builder

Five fields that everybody looks up every time. Type a cron expression or pick a schedule: you get what it means in plain words, when it runs next, the traps in it, and the same schedule as a systemd timer and as a Windows scheduled task.

the five fields

FieldValuesExamples
Minute0 to 590 on the hour, */15 every quarter, 5,35 twice an hour
Hour0 to 232 at night, 8-17 office hours, */6 four times a day
Day of month1 to 311 the first, 1,15 twice a month
Month1 to 12, or jan to dec* every month, 1,4,7,10 each quarter
Day of week0 to 7 (0 and 7 are Sunday), or sun to sat1-5 Monday to Friday, 0 Sunday

Reads the classic five-field cron of Linux and macOS, with lists, ranges, steps, names and the @daily style shortcuts. The six- and seven-field forms of Quartz, Spring and cloud schedulers are different dialects. The next runs are worked out in your browser's time zone.

poe-budget-calculator

Can the switch feed everything that is plugged into it? Enter the PoE budget from the data sheet and how many devices of each class it has to power.

the standards

NameStandardAt the switch portArrives at the deviceTypical devices
PoE802.3af, Type 115.4 W12.95 WPhones, simple cameras, older access points
PoE+802.3at, Type 230 W25.5 WCurrent access points, PTZ cameras
PoE++802.3bt, Type 360 W51 WWi-Fi 6E and 7 access points, thin clients
PoE++802.3bt, Type 490 W71.3 WDisplays, small switches, lighting

The difference between the two power columns is lost as heat in up to 100 m of cable. The class of a device is in its data sheet, often as "802.3at" or "PoE+ required". "Passive PoE" on some cameras and radio links is not part of these standards: it puts a fixed voltage on the cable without asking, and can damage a device that does not expect it. A switch's total budget is usually well below ports times 30 W: a 24-port PoE+ switch with 370 W can run twelve ports at full power, not twenty-four.

status-center

There's no free, keyless API for the official Microsoft 365 service status — the real one lives behind a login in the admin center. So this page does two honest things instead: quick links straight to the official status pages, and a live check of whether your own browser can currently reach the endpoints admins hit most.

official status pages

connectivity check

Tests whether your browser can reach each endpoint right now. A failure here can mean the service is down — but just as often it means a firewall, proxy, ad blocker or DNS filter on your side is blocking it. This is not an official outage indicator; for that, use the links above.

Checks run entirely in your browser using your own network connection. Nothing is sent to admin_hub or logged anywhere.

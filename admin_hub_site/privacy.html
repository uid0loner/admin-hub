2. Overview

You can use this site without registering. The tools run in your browser; what you enter into them is not sent to us.

3. Hosting and server log files

This site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. When you visit, the server processes technical data such as IP address, date and time, requested page and browser type to deliver and secure the site. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in stable and secure operation.

4. Storage of your cookie choice

We save your consent choice in your browser (local storage, key ah_consent) so we do not ask again. This is technically necessary to respect your decision. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR and Section 25 (2) TDDDG.

5. Tools

Text and files you put into the tools are processed locally in your browser and not transmitted to us. Do not enter secrets or data you are not allowed to process.

Two tools contact other servers, and only when you start them yourself. The DNS lookup and the domain check send the domain name you enter to the resolver you choose (Cloudflare or Google) over DNS-over-HTTPS. The connectivity check in the status center requests a small file from several Microsoft endpoints to see whether your browser can reach them. The breach check in the password strength tool sends the first five characters of the SHA-1 hash of your password to api.pwnedpasswords.com (Have I Been Pwned); the password itself and its full hash are not sent. In all these cases those providers receive your IP address, as with any web request. Nothing is sent to us.

6. Fonts

This site does not load fonts from external servers. It uses fonts that are already installed on your device.

7. Advertising

Ads are shown only after you consent. You can withdraw consent at any time under cookie settings in the footer.

8. Language and translation

The site is written in English. If you choose another language in the header, your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage key ah_lang) so the next page opens in the same language; translated phrases are kept in sessionStorage until you close the tab. The translation itself is done on your device by the translation function built into your browser (Chrome or Edge on desktop). The browser may download a language pack from its vendor for this; the text of the page is not sent to us or to anyone else. If your browser has no such function, we show a link to Google Translate. Google is only contacted if you click that link, and then receives the address of the page. Legal basis for storing the choice: Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG.

8a. Cockpit

The cockpit is off by default. If you switch on "Remember my results on this device", the tools save a short summary of each result (for example the domain you checked, a grade, a count and the titles of findings) in your browser's localStorage under the keys ah_cockpit and ah_cockpit_on. Log files, policies and user names you analyse are not saved. The data stays in your browser and is not transmitted to us. Switching the option off, or pressing "Forget everything", deletes it. The same applies to the display option ah_crt. Legal basis: Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG.

9. Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your message and email address to answer you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if it concerns a contract.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and you can withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). You can complain to a data protection authority, e.g. the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Bremen, which is responsible for Bremerhaven.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Last updated: September 2026.

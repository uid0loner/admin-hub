2. Overview

You can use this site without registering.

Most tools on this site run entirely in your browser. Text, files and other data you enter into these tools are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to us, except where a tool explicitly contacts an external service as described in section 5.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking technologies.

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is provided using services from Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the site, Cloudflare processes technical connection data required to deliver and protect the website. This may include your IP address, date and time of access, the requested page, browser and device information and similar technical data.

The purpose of this processing is to deliver the website, maintain its security, prevent abuse and ensure reliable operation. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure, reliable and efficient operation of this website.

Cloudflare acts as a service provider for the delivery and protection of the website. We do not maintain separate visitor profiles from these connection data. Technical logs are retained only for as long as necessary for security, troubleshooting and operation of the service, subject to the retention periods applicable to the Cloudflare services used.

Cloudflare may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. According to Cloudflare, international transfers are protected, where applicable, by mechanisms including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Cookies, local storage and offline cache

This site does not set cookies and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking technologies. For this reason, no consent banner is displayed.

Some functions store small amounts of information in your browser's localStorage or sessionStorage. This is done only where necessary to provide a function you have requested. The legal basis for storing or reading this information on your device is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

Data in sessionStorage is normally removed when you close the browser tab. Data in localStorage remains on your device until it is deleted by the site, by you through a provided control, or through your browser settings. None of it is transmitted to us.

5. Tools and local processing

Unless stated otherwise below, text, files and other information that you enter into the tools are processed locally in your browser and are not transmitted to us or to another server.

Please do not enter secrets, confidential information or personal data that you are not authorised to process.

The following tools contact external services, and only when you actively start the relevant function.

5.1 DNS lookup, domain check and DMARC report reader

The DNS lookup and the domain check send the domain name you enter to a public DNS resolver using DNS-over-HTTPS: Cloudflare DNS (cloudflare-dns.com, Cloudflare, Inc.) or Google Public DNS (dns.google, Google LLC). In the DNS lookup you choose the resolver.

In the DMARC report reader, the button that looks up host names sends the IP addresses of the sending servers listed in your report to Cloudflare DNS. The report itself is not transmitted.

The provider receives the requested name or address and the technical connection data, such as your IP address, that is necessarily transmitted when your browser connects to it. The purpose is solely to perform the lookup you requested. Nothing from these requests is sent to us.

The provider processes the request under its own privacy terms and retention policies. Depending on the provider, processing may take place outside the European Economic Area.

5.2 Password breach check

The breach check in the password strength tool uses the Pwned Passwords service of Have I Been Pwned.

Your password itself is never transmitted. It is hashed locally in your browser using SHA-1. Only the first five characters of that hash are sent to api.pwnedpasswords.com. The service returns possible matching hash endings, and the comparison is performed locally in your browser. This method is designed so that the provider receives neither your password nor its complete hash.

When the request is made, the provider also receives technical connection data such as your IP address. Have I Been Pwned is operated by Superlative Enterprises Pty Ltd, Queensland, Australia. Processing may therefore involve a transfer outside the European Economic Area.

The check is made only when you use this function. Nothing from this request is sent to us.

5.3 Connectivity check

The connectivity check in the status center requests small test resources from several Microsoft endpoints (among them login.microsoftonline.com, outlook.office365.com, graph.microsoft.com, teams.microsoft.com, www.office.com and portal.azure.com) to determine whether your browser can reach them.

When you start the check, your browser connects directly to these Microsoft servers. Microsoft therefore receives technical connection data such as your IP address and browser information. No files or content that you entered into admin_hub are transmitted as part of this check.

The check is made only when you start it. Nothing from these requests is sent to us. Microsoft may process data outside the European Economic Area in accordance with its own privacy terms and applicable transfer safeguards.

5.4 News and external links

The headlines on the news page and the list of exploited vulnerabilities are collected from public feeds by an automated job on the server side and published as part of this site. Your browser does not contact those sources when you read the page.

The site contains many links to external websites. An external site receives data only when you follow such a link.

6. Fonts

This site does not load fonts from external font services. IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono are provided directly by this website. Your browser therefore does not contact Google Fonts or another external font provider in order to display them.

7. Advertising, analytics and tracking

This website contains no advertising. We do not use advertising networks, visitor analytics, tracking pixels, behavioural profiling or cross-site tracking. We do not create advertising profiles about visitors.

8. Language and translation

The website is written in English. If you choose another language in the header, your selection is stored in your browser under the localStorage key ah_lang so that the following pages use the same language. Translated phrases are kept in sessionStorage until you close the tab.

The translation is performed on your device by a translation function provided by supported browsers such as Chrome or Edge on desktop. Depending on your browser, it may download a language pack from the browser vendor. The text of the page is not sent to us as part of this process.

If your browser does not provide this function, the site shows a link to Google Translate. Google is contacted only if you open that link. In that case, Google receives at least the address of the page and normal technical connection data.

The legal basis for storing or reading this information on your device is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

9. Cockpit and saved lists

The cockpit is disabled by default. If you enable "Remember my results on this device", some tools store a short summary of their results in your browser's localStorage (ah_cockpit, ah_cockpit_on). This may include a domain you checked, a result grade, result counts and titles of findings. Log files, policies and user names that you analyse are not stored by the cockpit.

You can delete the stored summaries with "Forget everything", by switching the feature off, or by deleting the site's data in your browser.

The asset inventory and the licence tracker work the same way: only if you tick "Keep the list in this browser" is the list you entered stored in localStorage, and unticking the box deletes it. Such a list may contain personal data that you enter yourself, for example the names of users of devices.

This information remains in your browser and is not transmitted to us. The legal basis for storing or reading this information on your device is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

ntfs-share-permissions-cheat-sheet

Two sets of permissions apply to a file reached over the network: those on the share and those on the folder (NTFS). The stricter one wins. The usual setup leaves the share wide open for authenticated users and does all the work with NTFS. D:\Data and CORP are placeholders.

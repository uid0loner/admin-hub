dns-lookup

Resolves records directly from your browser via DNS-over-HTTPS — no DNS tool, no server-side proxy on admin_hub, nothing logged. Pick a public resolver, pick a record type, and the query goes straight from your device to that resolver.

DNS-over-HTTPS means the query itself travels as a normal HTTPS request to the resolver you picked, not as plaintext UDP on port 53 — so it isn't visible to anyone sniffing local network traffic, though your chosen resolver (Cloudflare or Google) does see it. Results come from that resolver's cache and reflect what it currently has, which can differ briefly from the authoritative server during propagation after a change.

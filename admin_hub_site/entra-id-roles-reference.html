entra-id-roles-reference

The most commonly assigned Entra ID directory roles and what they can actually do. Search by name or keyword.

Roles change over time and Microsoft adds new ones. This covers the roles admins meet most often, not the full list. Prefer the least powerful role that does the job, and use Privileged Identity Management for just-in-time access.

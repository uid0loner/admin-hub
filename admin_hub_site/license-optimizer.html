license-optimizer

Drop an export of your users and their Microsoft 365 licences. You see how much of the monthly bill pays for nothing: licences on blocked accounts, licences already covered by a bigger one, accounts nobody uses and seats assigned to no one. The file is read by your browser and never uploaded.

Each licence of a user is counted once, in the order of the table. The money figures use the prices in the table on the result, which start as rough list prices and are yours to correct. A licence you remove from a user becomes an unassigned seat first. The bill only shrinks when you reduce the seat count, which on an annual term is possible at renewal. The page knows what the common suites and add-ons contain. It does not know how much a person actually uses a product, so it cannot tell you who could move from E5 to E3. Products it does not recognise are listed with a price of zero until you enter one.

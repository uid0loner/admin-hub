about

admin_hub is built by an IT technician who works with Microsoft 365, Entra ID and security every day, and wanted one place for the things that are otherwise scattered across bookmarks, old scripts and paid tools.

what it is

A workbench for people who run IT: analyzers for sign-in logs, Conditional Access policies and mail domains, an interactive attack map, an incident simulator, calculators and generators, cheat sheets, checklists, a glossary and a news feed. The home page sorts it by what you are trying to do. The start-here page sorts it by who you are.

how it works

  • Your data stays with you. The tools run in your browser. Logs, policies and passwords you put in are not uploaded. The few tools that contact another server (DNS lookups, the breach check, the connectivity check) say so next to the button, and the privacy page lists them.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for. The optional cockpit keeps your results in your own browser and nowhere else.
  • Works offline. After the first visit the pages are cached, so the reference material and most tools work without a connection.
  • Checkable. Where a page states facts that change, such as end-of-support dates or MITRE technique IDs, it links to the primary source so you can verify them.
  • Honest about limits. The analyzers produce leads, not verdicts, and each one says what it cannot see.

getting around

Press Ctrl K anywhere to search, or to paste an IP address, a domain, an error code, a token or a timestamp and get an answer on the spot. Press ? for the keyboard shortcuts. The language button in the header translates a page on your own device in Chrome and Edge on desktop.

how it stays current

The news and exploited vulnerabilities pages are rebuilt automatically every few hours from public feeds, with links to the original sources. Everything else is written and checked by hand.

mistakes

There will be some. If you find a wrong error code, a broken command or a tool that gives a bad answer, get in touch.

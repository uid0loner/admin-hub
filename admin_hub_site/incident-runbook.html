It's happening right now and you need steps, not theory. Pick what you're dealing with, answer a couple of questions, and get a runbook built for your situation — ordered, checkable, printable.

A generated starting point based on common practice, not a substitute for your own incident response plan, your insurer's requirements, or legal advice on breach notification. For a fixed reference instead of a generator, see the compromised account checklist or backup & disaster recovery checklist.

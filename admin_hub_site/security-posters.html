security-posters

Awareness training is forgotten in a week. A sheet of paper next to the coffee machine is read every day. Ten posters, each with one message and four rules. Pick the ones you want, add who to call, and print: one poster per page, black on white, no logo and no stock photo of a hacker in a hoodie.

preview

Print on A4 or Letter, portrait. In the print dialog, switch off headers and footers, and "fit to page" if your printer cuts the edges. Swap the posters every month or two: a poster that has hung for a year is wallpaper. Nothing you type is stored or sent.
